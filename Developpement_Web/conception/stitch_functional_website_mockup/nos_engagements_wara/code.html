Our Commitment

From Plague to Premium Material.

In Guadeloupe, sargassum is an environmental crisis. We view it as an opportunity. A regenerative journey to transform the ocean's burden into the earth's future textile.

waves The Crisis

A Smothered Coastline

Millions of tons of Sargassum seaweed wash ashore in the Caribbean annually, devastating local marine ecosystems and coastal economies. As it decomposes on beaches, it releases toxic hydrogen sulfide gas.

WARA was born from the urgent need to address this ecological disaster in Guadeloupe, turning a massive waste problem into a sustainable resource.

24M+ Tons Of Sargassum biomass recorded in the Great Atlantic Belt annually.

The Regenerative Process

Our proprietary extraction technology isolates biopolymers from raw sargassum without harmful chemicals, spinning them into durable, biodegradable fibers.

01

Harvest

Ethical collection directly from the shores of Guadeloupe, preventing shoreline decomposition and protecting local flora.

02

Extraction

A closed-loop, cold-water process extracts alginate biopolymers while recovering fresh water for agricultural reuse.

03

Spinning

The purified polymer is wet-spun into a continuous filament, creating a premium yarn ready for high-end textile weaving.
